Projects

Applied learning in data and research

01

Data Analysis for Bank Retail Business

Based on seven business tables in a MySQL database, including a customer information table and a loan account information table, I used FineBI to build relationships between tables and completed an analysis of the balance point between retail loan marketing and risk control.

02

Macroeconomic Data Interpretation

I completed data organisation and structural analysis for the investment and consumption modules in the first half of 2026, and solved many problems in the process. For example, on the consumption side, I compared the difference between the growth rate of total retail sales of consumer goods and the broader services retail caliber, and found that the 5.3% growth in services retail sales was the main driver.

This helped me understand that sustained growth in services consumption can offset part of the downward pressure on goods consumption. I also learned that when looking at data, I should not only look at the surface numbers, but also think clearly about what factors behind them may be distorting the picture.

03

Research on China's Economic Structural Transformation

This experience was the first time I went through the full process of economic research, from concept definition to data verification and then to policy analysis. My group members and I focused on analysing the share of secondary industry and tertiary industry value added in GDP.

We collected data on household income, imports and exports, and other indicators from the National Bureau of Statistics and the World Bank, and used Excel to complete trend analysis and indicator calculations.
